Flagship platform · Open source

SAMSustainable Analytical Model

Building geometry, engineering assumptions and simulation often live in separate tools. SAM connects them through a reusable analytical building model, supporting building physics, dynamic thermal simulation, compliance-oriented workflows and HVAC analysis.

My contribution: engineering content and technical product direction, from requirements and data logic to user journeys, acceptance criteria and validation. I work with developers on implementation and debugging, and support engineers through pilots, training and workflow refinement.

The result: a connected platform for model generation, attribution, simulation and HVAC scenario comparison, with shared data and repeatable validation.

02Psychrometrics & HVAC

SAM_Mollier

Air-conditioning processes need a clear connection between moist-air properties, charts and HVAC loads. SAM_Mollier brings psychrometric calculations and air-process exploration into the SAM ecosystem.

My contribution: engineering methods and workflow definition within SAM, alongside co-authored research on the open-source tool and its use in HVAC analysis.

Supports chart-based exploration of air processes and concept-stage HVAC design.

03Interoperability

BIM-to-BEM workflows

A design model needs usable geometry, engineering attributes and reliable relationships before it can become a building energy model.

My contribution: defining workflows across Revit, Rhino/Grasshopper, IFC, gbXML and simulation tools, with an emphasis on reusable analytical models, traceable identity and validation.

Connects BIM inputs to simulation-ready models. Tas is the primary simulation pathway; native OpenStudio conversion is in development.

◌ Digital twins are an area of interest, not a current SAM capability.

04Earlier work · Buro Happold

Single Analysis Model
& BHoM Building Environments

Repeating the same engineering setup across design and simulation tools created opportunities for a shared computational approach.

My contribution: developing the earlier Single Analysis Model approach at Buro Happold and later leading discipline-side Building Environments workflows for BHoM, translating MEP and building-physics requirements into reusable methods.

A progression from project prototypes to shared engineering toolkits. Buro Happold received the Innovation award for the Single Analysis Model.

05Voluntary open source

Spider gbXML Viewer
/ Ladybug Tools
